TikTok Unfollow Check

See who you follow that does not follow you back. You use your own TikTok data file. No password, no login, and your file never leaves your device.

1. Get your data from TikTok

  1. In the TikTok app: Profile → menu (☰) → Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data.
  2. Choose Select data to download and tick only Profile and Settings. You do not need "Your Activity": it is much bigger and holds your watch history. Choose the JSON file format.
  3. Tap Request data. TikTok prepares it in the background; it can take from a few hours to a few days.
  4. When it is ready, go back to the same screen, tap Download, and save the file (a zip).

The download link expires after a few days. Use a computer or phone browser to upload the file below. You can upload the zip as it is, or the unzipped user_data.json / text files.

These numbers can differ from what you see in TikTok. The file is a copy made at one moment, and TikTok may leave some people out of it. This can happen with any account, and most often when you have more than 10,000 followers, so the followers and "Fans" lists can be incomplete, and a few people shown as "not following back" may actually follow you.

Compare the counts above with your TikTok profile. If they look far apart, request a new export after some time and upload it again; if you upload both files together, the page merges them. Before you unfollow someone, you can open their profile to check.

Your file has close to 10,000 followers, which is where TikTok is reported to cut the list in its export. If your profile shows more followers than that, the list is incomplete.

Attention: respect TikTok's limits. TikTok limits how many accounts you can follow or unfollow in a short time. If you go too fast, it may warn you or block the action.

  • Take breaks between unfollows, and between your sessions with this page. Do not use it for hours in a row.
  • Work in small batches, and stop for the day if TikTok shows a warning.
  • This page never unfollows for you. You unfollow yourself in TikTok, at your own pace.
